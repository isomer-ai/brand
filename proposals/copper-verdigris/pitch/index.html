    In production · a large commercial carrier

    The flag lands before the suit

    In time means the handler knows while the deadline can still be met and before a suit is filed. For seven weeks at a large commercial carrier, Isomer read every new claim on arrival, about 3,000 of them. Here is what that looked like.

    What in time looks like

    A demand to tender the full $500,000 policy limit, due by noon, arrived as part 1 of a 16-part package on a file no one had worked yet. Isomer flagged it critical when part 1 arrived.

    It is rarely on page one. 18% of flags came from page 31 or deeper.

    Liability claims handler
