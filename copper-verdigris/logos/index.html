Isomer logos

Black and white logos for the Copper Verdigris palette, each shown on the ground it's made for. Every file has a transparent background, so the white logos can look blank until they're on a dark ground. Download one file at a time: SVG for screens and anything that scales, PNG where SVG isn't accepted.

Horizontal

The default. Mastheads, slide corners, email headers, the site.

Vertical

Square and tall slots: covers, event signage, merchandise.

Mark

Small and square: app icon, favicon, avatar, slide footers.

Use

  • Ink on paper or white; white on ink or graphite. Never the color logos with this palette.
  • Every file has a transparent background; the paper and ink behind them on the logo board are only the preview. The white files can look blank in a file preview or on a light page because they are white on transparent. Place them on ink or graphite.
  • The logo is not a device: it doesn't count toward one colored device per view, and it never takes copper or verdigris.
  • The ink files are the solid-dark logos recolored from Isomer Blue #000441 to ink #141416; shapes are unchanged. The white files are the solid-light logos.
  • Product screens use the logo in the heading color: ink on light, white on dark.
  • The files already include the guideline's minimum clear space: about ½A around the horizontal logo and ⅓A beside the mark, where A is the mark's width. Don't crop it; give the logo more room where you can.
Don'tRecolor the logo copper, verdigris, or any color but ink and white.
Don'tStretch, rotate, outline, or add a shadow or gradient.
Don'tPlace the ink logo on ink or graphite, or the white logo on paper.
